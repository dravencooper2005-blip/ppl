Weekly PPL

How it works

  • Log weight and reps for every set, then tap the set number when it's done. The rest timer starts.
  • Last week's numbers show under each lift. Beat them by a rep or two.
  • When every set hits the top of the rep range, you get an "Add weight" flag. Add a little weight and start over at the bottom.
  • Compounds stop 1–2 reps shy of failure. Isolations go to failure, with partials on the last set of machines and cables.
  • Deload every 6–8 weeks, or when lifts stall: halve your sets for a week.

Weekly sets per muscle in weeks 1–4, 120 total. Indirect work counts as half a set.
